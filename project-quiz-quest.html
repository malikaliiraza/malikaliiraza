Quiz Quest

A colorful terminal-based quiz game in pure Python, inspired by Who Wants to Be a Millionaire. Answer 16 multiple-choice questions across 3 rounds of increasing difficulty to win up to Rs. 150,000 – with secured prize tiers, three lifelines, and the option to quit anytime and keep your secured amount.

View repo on GitHub

Overview

  • 16 multiple-choice questions on science, history, geography, Islamic history, art, and pop culture
  • Three rounds of increasing difficulty with secured prize tiers locked in at the end of each round
  • Round 1 — Easy: 8 questions at Rs. 5,000 each (Rs. 40,000 total)
  • Round 2 — Medium: 5 questions at Rs. 10,000 each (Rs. 50,000 total); secured amount Rs. 90,000
  • Round 3 — Final (Hard): 3 questions at Rs. 20,000 each (Rs. 60,000 total); secured amount Rs. 150,000
  • Three lifelines, one use each per game: 50/50 removes two incorrect options; Audience Poll shows a simulated audience vote as percentages; Skip Question skips without winning or losing money
  • Quit anytime and keep the secured amount; end-of-game report; replay without restarting
  • Colorful terminal interface using ANSI escape codes, with input validation
  • Pure Python — standard library only, nothing to install; requires Python 3.6+ and a terminal with ANSI color support
